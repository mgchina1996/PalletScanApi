PalletScan Staff Guide

Web guide for recording Cartons and TPINs on mobile phones and tablets

1. Select a location

  1. Open Locations and search for the location, such as S1-A13.
  2. Select the complete location code.
  3. Check Selected location, then tap Start.

Use Done, the keyboard Search/Enter key, or select a location to close the mobile keyboard.

Always confirm that the selected location matches your physical pallet location.

2. Record a Carton

  1. Select Carton and tap Take Photo or Choose Photo.
  2. Keep the complete Carton Number clear and inside the frame.
  3. Select the correct result and tap Confirm Carton.
  4. If no result is correct, tap Enter Manually, enter the complete Carton Number, and confirm it.
  5. Enter at least one counted Actual quantity.
  6. Tap Save Carton in the fixed bottom bar.
Blank products are not submitted. Every entered quantity must be a whole number greater than 0.

3. Record a TPIN

  1. Select TPIN and tap Take Photo or Choose Photo.
  2. Keep the complete TPIN clear and inside the frame.
  3. Select the correct result and tap Confirm TPIN.
  4. If no result is correct, tap Enter Manually and enter the full TPIN.
  5. Enter the counted Actual quantity.
  6. Tap Save in the fixed bottom bar.
The quantity must be a whole number greater than 0.

4. After saving

Continue ScanningKeep the current location and scan another Carton or TPIN.
Complete LocationFinish this location and return to location selection.
When you move to another pallet location, always choose Complete Location and select the new location.

5. Records

Open Records from the bottom navigation to review saved entries.

  • Filter by All, Carton, or TPIN.
  • Search by Carton Number or TPIN.
  • Select an entry to review its location, time, products, and quantities.
